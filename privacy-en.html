Yushin Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 — 3 September 2026

Who we are

Yushin is a B2B educational and informational software application for professional practitioners and students of Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) and Japanese acupuncture. The application structures information entered by the user and shows traditional pattern relationships, theory, meridian connections, and reference information based on predefined rules. Yushin does not provide a medical diagnosis, does not perform medical screening or emergency triage, does not predict disease, and does not determine, recommend, or optimize medical or therapeutic treatment. The information displayed is not a substitute for a professional medical assessment or for the user’s own responsibility and professional competence.

Yushin is offered by DanYuZen CommV, registered office at Tervantstraat 257, 3583 Paal, Belgium, company number 1019.352.907, VAT number BE 1019.352.907, represented by Danny Currinckx. Website: www.danyuzen.com. Contact for privacy and security questions: info@danyuzen.com.

Which processing activities are we responsible for?

DanYuZen acts as data controller for account management of practice users, contract and billing data, and the necessary security logs of the service.

For patient data that your practice enters and processes via Yushin, DanYuZen acts as processor on behalf of your practice. Your practice remains the data controller for this data and informs its patients via its own privacy policy.

What data do we collect, and why?

  • Name, email address, and role of practice users — for account management.
  • Billing data — based on the legal bookkeeping obligation.
  • Technical security logs — for the security of the service, retained according to our retention policy.

For patient data entered into Yushin via your practice (patient label/pseudonym, answers to the TCM intake, complaint/symptom information, age/sex where functionally necessary, and the resulting educational pattern output), we process this data solely on the instructions of, and on behalf of, your practice.

Legal basis

For account and subscription management, we rely on the performance of the contract as the legal basis. For statutory administration and billing, we rely on the applicable legal obligation and/or performance of the contract. For necessary security logs and fraud prevention, we may rely on legitimate interest. For health data, we process solely on the instructions of your practice; your practice determines its own legal basis.

Who do we share data with?

We engage the following carefully selected service providers (sub-processors): Cloudflare (hosting/infrastructure), Mollie (payment processing), and Resend (transactional email, such as registration confirmations). We maintain an up-to-date list of sub-processors and impose appropriate data protection obligations on each one.

Where data is processed outside the European Economic Area, this only happens on the basis of a valid transfer mechanism (such as an adequacy decision or Standard Contractual Clauses).

How long do we keep data?

Data is retained for the duration of your subscription, plus a 30-calendar-day export window after termination of the agreement. After that, patient data is deleted, subject to legally mandated retention periods (e.g. for billing data).

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, transfer, and object with regard to your personal data. You can exercise these rights via info@danyuzen.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority — in Belgium, this is the Data Protection Authority (gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be).

Are you a patient of a practice that uses Yushin? For your own data, please contact your practice — they are the data controller for this purpose.

Security

We apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to secure personal data, including access management, multi-factor authentication for administrators, encrypted connections (TLS), separation of data per practice (tenant isolation), audit logging, backups, and an incident response procedure.

No sale of data

We do not sell personal data and do not use patient data for advertising or profiling.

Changes to this policy

Whenever we make a material change to this privacy policy, we update the version number and date at the top. Important changes are communicated via your account or by email.
